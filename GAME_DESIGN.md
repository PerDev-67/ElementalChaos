# Game design

## Campaign

Ren arrives at Iris Station as disturbances spread through nearby energy installations. Dr. Mira tracks the fractures, Tess makes field upgrades, Lumi helps identify the upper current, and Captain Orin keeps the station and Citadel evacuation route open. NPCs can be approached on foot. The map travels directly to the station and unlocked regions. First visits include skippable field briefings.

| Chapter | Mission | Battle | Element unlocked |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | First Awakening | Defend the vale relay and defeat the last attackers after 83 seconds of waves | Hydrogen |
| 1 | The Rising Current | Protect a raised installation through 127 seconds of faster and ranged attacks | Helium |
| 2 | Unstable Frontier | Secure the survey ring for a total of 22 uncontested seconds and defeat the 184-second assault | Lithium |
| 2 | The Broken Citadel | Hold against attacks from four entrances and stop the Citadel guardian in a 244-second defense | Beryllium |

Finishing each mission for the first time earns shards. First-clear rewards and unlocks persist. Replays let the player test the full roster, including Beryllium. The station shop sells four permanent traits. Battle ranks are bought with energy during a mission and reset afterward.

## Combat

Ren can walk, run, use a free short-range strike, spend energy on a larger Pulse, and use one field recovery per battle. Hostiles drop occasional energy crystals that can be collected by walking over them. Core loss ends the battle immediately.

Allies attack and move autonomously. Hydrogen is affordable and effective in groups. Helium can heal nearby allies, Lithium strikes additional enemies around its target, and Beryllium reduces damage to nearby allies. Tapping units toggles individual selection. Orders affect that selection, or the whole squad when no individual selection is active. Units can defend an anchor, follow Ren, pursue enemies, or move to a rally point.

In Level 3, Interact near the survey beacon to start a fictional 18 K cooling field. The HUD displays temperature and physical state. Hydrogen is treated as liquid at that temperature under the demonstration’s approximate pressure, and moves more slowly as a gameplay effect. The system uses approximate transition temperatures near one atmosphere. Real phase behavior depends on pressure and chemical species.

The station tutorial teaches movement, zoom, interaction, summoning, autonomous attacks, commands, core defense, and the mission goal. It is replayable from the title screen.

## Character direction

Ren is a separate male protagonist. The supporting cast leans female across the station and elemental roster. Portraits give each character a distinct appearance. Simple Mode uses colored spheres, and Stylized Adventurers uses light humanoid models. The four prototype element ages are fixed character data. A reusable age sampler supports a 1–100 range with most draws between 15 and 23 for future characters.
